Get involved

If you live in Whyalla, work here, grew up here or have left and still care about the city, we'd like to hear from you. Taking part is voluntary, and you can stop at any time.

01

Talk with us

A conversational interview, about an hour, at a time and place that suits you.

02

Join a workshop

A group session imagining what the city could be and what it needs.

03

Explore working together

Got an idea, a project or a question the research could help with? We're open to a chat about ways we might support it.

04

Stay in the loop

Be notified when research outputs are available, from new episodes to the final report.

Register your interest

Registering doesn't sign you up to anything. We'll send you the Explanatory Statement and you can decide from there.
