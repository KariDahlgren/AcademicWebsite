Hydrogen Hub Futures · Field site

Whyalla, South Australia

An industrial city on the Upper Spencer Gulf, home to a steelworks and to one of South Australia's flagship hydrogen proposals.

Whyalla brings together many of the dynamics at the heart of the hydrogen hub model: a long-standing heavy industry and workforce, state-led investment in hydrogen production, power generation and storage, and the promise that co-locating hydrogen supply with industrial demand could help decarbonise steelmaking.

It is also a place where hydrogen futures have been announced, revised and deferred, making it a revealing site for understanding how hub visions are lived, contested and imagined by the communities expected to host them.

Questions the project brings to Whyalla

  • How does imagining a region as a hub shape local expectations of jobs, growth and decarbonisation?
  • How do hydrogen hub visions relate to longer histories of industrial transformation?
  • When do speculative hydrogen futures accelerate decarbonisation, and when do they defer it?
