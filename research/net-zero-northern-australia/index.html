Research

Embedding Net Zero Carbon Emissions in Northern Australia

An ethnography of the professionals working in carbon emissions generation, mediation and mitigation in the Northern Territory.

Years
2025–2028
Funder
Australian Research Council, Discovery Project
Team
Timothy Neale, Matthew Kearnes, Tess Lea, Chris Mayes, Gisa Weszkalnys

The project grows out of an ongoing collaboration interrogating circular carbon management. It follows the entanglements of the people whose work generates, mediates and mitigates carbon emissions in the Northern Territory, and how net zero commitments are embedded in practice.
