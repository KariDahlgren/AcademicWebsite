Take part

Your experience of Whyalla matters to this research. Here is what taking part involves and how to get in touch.

Who can take part

I would like to hear from a wide range of people, including:

  • people who live in Whyalla or the Upper Spencer Gulf
  • current and former steelworks, mining and industry workers, and their families
  • people working in local government, business, education, health or community groups
  • anyone with a view on Whyalla’s future, whether hopeful, worried or both

You don’t need any special knowledge of hydrogen or energy.

What it involves

Conversation
An informal interview of about an hour, at a time and place that suits you, or by phone or video.
Podcast
If you would like to, you can share your story on the Voices of Whyalla podcast. This is separate and entirely optional.
Your choice
Taking part is voluntary. You can skip any question, and you can withdraw.
Privacy
You can choose to stay anonymous. Your information is stored securely and handled under Monash University ethics requirements.
