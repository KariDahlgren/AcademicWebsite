What taking part involves

  • Talk with us: a conversational interview of about an hour, at a time and place that suits you.
  • Join a workshop: a group session imagining what the town could be and what it needs.
  • Explore working together: an informal chat about ideas or projects the research might support.

Your choice to take part

Taking part is entirely voluntary. You don't have to answer any question you don't want to, and you can stop at any time without giving a reason.

You can withdraw your information up until [withdrawal deadline, e.g. publication or a set date].

Privacy & your information

Unless you choose to be named, we will not use your name in anything we publish, and we will remove details that could identify you.

Your information will be stored securely on Monash University systems and only the research team will have access to it.

Recording & the podcast

Interviews may be audio-recorded, with your permission, so we can accurately capture what you say. You can ask for the recorder to be turned off at any time.

Appearing in Voices from Whyalla or any film is a separate choice, with its own consent. Taking part in the research doesn't mean you'll be filmed.
