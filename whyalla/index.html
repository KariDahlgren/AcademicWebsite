Whyalla is where the challenge of decarbonising heavy industry meets a town that's heard many promises. What futures do people here want, for themselves and their community?

Research questions

This project uses ethnography, futures methods and co-design workshops to understand how people in Whyalla imagine their futures. The path to decarbonising iron and steel making is uncertain, and the people who live with that uncertainty deserve a say in what comes next.

RQ1

Who imagines Whyalla's future?

How are futures for the town produced by governments, companies and investors, and what models, like the hydrogen hub, do they rely on?

RQ2

How are promises lived?

How do residents experience repeated cycles of announcement, hope, delay and insecurity, and how does that history shape trust in the next plan?

RQ3

What does decarbonising steel mean here?

What are the everyday stakes of industrial decarbonisation for jobs, health, landscape and identity in a steel town?

RQ4

Whose futures are missing?

Whose voices and desires are absent from top-down approaches to regional industrial town planning, and what do they imagine instead?

    Get involved

    If you live in Whyalla, work here, grew up here or have left and still care about the town, we'd like to hear from you. Taking part is voluntary, and you can stop at any time.

    01

    Talk with us

    A conversational interview, about an hour, at a time and place that suits you.

    02

    Join a workshop

    A group session imagining what the town could be and what it needs.

    03

    Explore working together

    Got an idea, a project or a question the research could help with? We're open to a chat about ways we might support it.

    04

    Stay in the loop

    Be notified when research outputs are available, from new episodes to the final report.
